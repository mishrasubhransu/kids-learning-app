# feat: Recap sticker — end-of-session payoff with per-child sticker shelf

**Date:** 2026-07-29 11:03

## Summary

Finishing any test or game now ends with a recap moment: a full-screen
collage of what the child just saw circle-reveals over the finished game
(the lesson intro machinery in reverse), a spoken line celebrates the
session, and a sticker pops in. Stickers persist per child and pile up on
a sticker shelf on the home page — toddler-appropriate progress with no
numbers or streaks.

## Added
- `SessionRecap` overlay (`src/components/ui/SessionRecap.jsx`): reverse
  circle-reveal, polaroid collage of the session's items, TTS recap line,
  sticker award with pop animation; tap / right arrow / space dismisses
- `src/lib/stickers.js`: sticker pool, per-child persistence helpers
  (JSON in the settings jsonb under `stickers`, capped at 200), recap
  lines with the no-repeat rule from `pickIntro`
- `src/lib/recapTiles.js`: per-category collage tiles for TestingMode
  (letters, numbers, color swatches, shape SVGs, phonics onset/rime,
  photos, action videos)
- `StickerShelf` on Home: shows the active child's stickers (last 14 +
  count), hidden until the first sticker is earned
- `sticker_earned` analytics event (category, mode, sticker emoji)

## Changed
- TestingMode, MatchGame, SceneQuiz, and TypingMode's test all show the
  recap over their existing trophy screens; the trophy screen with Start
  Again remains behind it
- MatchGame's spoken completion line moved into the recap (was "Great
  job! You found N opposites!")
- TestingMode's ArrowRight advance is disabled once the test is complete
  so the arrow belongs to the recap (prevented a double sticker award)
